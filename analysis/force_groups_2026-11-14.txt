6. Octonion check (Hurwitz 1898; Cartan 1914; Gunaydin & Gursey 1973)
   all symmetries of the octonions: dimension 14 (G2 = 14)
   symmetries that keep one direction (e7) fixed: dimension 8
   rank (neutral generators) = 2 -> dimension 8, rank 2 = SU(3)
   they all commute with 'multiply by e7' on the other 6 directions: max error 6.4e-16 -> the 6 directions are 3 COMPLEX ones = 3 colours;
   the real axis and e7 are untouched = 1 colourless (lepton-like) slot.  8 = 1 + 1 + 3 + 3bar

7. Glue between the three groups (the 'Z6' condition)
