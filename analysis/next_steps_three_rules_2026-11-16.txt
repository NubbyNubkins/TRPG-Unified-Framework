=== Rule 1: the facing u acts on the far side by commutator [u,x] ===
  e0: |[u,e0]| = 0
  e1: |[u,e1]| = 0
  e2: |[u,e2]| = 2
  e3: |[u,e3]| = 2
  e4: |[u,e4]| = 2
  e5: |[u,e5]| = 2
  e6: |[u,e6]| = 2
  e7: |[u,e7]| = 2
  random x: |[u,x]|^2 = 19.5307 = 4 x |coloured part|^2 = 19.5307
  -> zero on exactly the colourless parts {1,u} (the MMG parts, plus the real diagonal b,c), equal non-zero on all 6 coloured parts (D).
  Exact (octonion algebra; colour = stabiliser of u, Gunaydin-Gursey 1973). Same pattern as matter number (D +-2/3, MMG 0).
  The weak force forbids any term weighing only some MMG parts, so the only far-side weights are |D|^2 and |MMG|^2.
  The spot is a STATE (the hidden partner, an earlier test), not a setting: the determinant term a(bc-|x|^2) gives no weight -> |MMG|^2 coefficient 0.
  Result: D weighs F (facing term), MMG only via the grain: F^2/grain = 2.409e+08 GeV.
