2. Lined up by the equilibrium (balance) point with the proton

  The balance mass of any particle with the proton is m p/(m + p). As a fraction of the proton: phi = 1/(1 + exp(-2S)), with S the size from
  the proton. That is a logistic (S-shaped) curve: everything far lighter sits at its own mass, everything far heavier is capped at the proton,
  and the proton paired with itself sits exactly at one half.

3. The eigen equations applied to the size and link matrices themselves
========================================================================================================
  Link matrix, 9 charged matter particles: only 2 non-zero eigenvalues: [-682.247, 700.247]
     formula n +- sqrt(sum of masses x sum of 1/masses) = 700.247, -682.247
  Link matrix, all 16: only 2 non-zero eigenvalues: [-37306206.436, 37306238.436]
     formula n +- sqrt(sum of masses x sum of 1/masses) = 37306238.436, -37306206.436
  For the charged particles: the top carries 96% of the sum of masses; the electron 74% of the sum of 1/masses
  (up quark 17%, down 8%). So the big eigenvalue, 700, is mostly the electron-top link value (581)
  stretched 1.19 times by the light quarks. The matrix's eigen-structure is set mainly by its two ends.
  Size matrix (all 16): eigenvalues are 0 (14 times) and +-i x 86.11: only the spread of the log masses survives.
  So the eigen equations confirm last night's muse in exact form: the matrix of relationships is governed by its lightest and heaviest member.
  They cannot produce the masses: both matrices are built from the masses and have only two directions (rank 2).

4. The cone (circulant eigen) equation applied to the sizes instead of the square-root masses
========================================================================================================
  Particles: sqrt(mass) = a (1 + b cos(theta + 2 pi g/3)); for the leptons b = sqrt2 (the 45 degrees). Sizes: S_g = A + B cos(theta + 2 pi g/3), S from the proton.
  leptons    sizes: centre  -1.510, swing  2.391, angle  160.07 deg  |  sqrt-mass cone: swing/centre 1.4142 (sqrt2 = 1.4142), angle  132.73 deg
  up type    sizes: centre  -0.092, swing  3.268, angle  154.30 deg  |  sqrt-mass cone: swing/centre 1.7586 (sqrt2 = 1.4142), angle  124.27 deg
  down type  sizes: centre  -1.018, swing  1.965, angle  146.06 deg  |  sqrt-mass cone: swing/centre 1.5452 (sqrt2 = 1.4142), angle  126.31 deg
  neutrinos  sizes: centre -12.941, swing  1.446, angle  159.42 deg  |  sqrt-mass cone: swing/centre 1.4137 (sqrt2 = 1.4142), angle  147.38 deg
  Every family fits (three numbers, three free values), so a fit alone says nothing. The sizes' angles differ family to family,
  so on sizes there is no shared cone. The square-root form stays the only one where a family (leptons, and neutrinos by construction) is exact.
