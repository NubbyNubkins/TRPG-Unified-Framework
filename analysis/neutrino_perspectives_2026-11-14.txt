  JUNO/reactor 3 MeV, 53 km    oscillation phase (31)   56.72 rad, (21)  1.677 rad
  KamLAND 3 MeV, 180 km        oscillation phase (31)  192.63 rad, (21)  5.694 rad

If the three masses were equal (one mass, three viewpoints only), every phase above is 0: no oscillation.
Oscillation is seen, with the L/E dip (Super-K 2004, KamLAND 2008). So the three mass values are real and different.
What CAN be one object: the neutrino is one field with a 3-state inside; flavours are views of it (this is standard).

D. Does neutrino mass depend on temperature or activity (energy)?
==============================================================================
  KamLAND reactor (~3 MeV, core ~600 K)    dm21 = 7.530e-05 +/- 1.8e-06 eV^2  (typical E 0.003 GeV)
  Solar (0.1-15 MeV, core 15 million K)    dm21 = 6.100e-05 +/- 1.2e-05 eV^2  (typical E 0.008 GeV)
  Daya Bay reactor (~4 MeV)                dm31 = 2.519e-03 +/- 6.0e-05 eV^2  (typical E 0.004 GeV)
  T2K/NOvA accelerator (0.6-2 GeV)         dm31 = 2.520e-03 +/- 4.0e-05 eV^2  (typical E 1.5 GeV)
  Super-K atmospheric (0.1-100 GeV)        dm31 = 2.500e-03 +/- 1.0e-04 eV^2  (typical E 5.0 GeV)
  IceCube DeepCore (6-60 GeV)              dm31 = 2.480e-03 +/- 6.0e-05 eV^2  (typical E 25.0 GeV)
  fit dm31 proportional to E^p across 4 MeV - 25 GeV: p = -0.0013 +/- 0.0037
  -> mass^2 changes by at most 6.7% over a factor 6250 in energy (2 sigma)
  The solar value sits about 1.2 sigma below KamLAND: consistent (it was ~2 sigma before 2020).
Verdict: the vacuum mass splits do NOT depend on source temperature or neutrino energy.

What DOES change with surroundings: the MSW matter effect (Wolfenstein 1978; Mikheyev & Smirnov 1985).
  solar core (rho ~150 g/cm3) : extra potential V = 7.60e-12 eV; solar-split resonance energy = 1.9 MeV
  Earth's mantle              : extra potential V = 1.90e-13 eV; solar-split resonance energy = 76.0 MeV
  outer Sun                   : extra potential V = 3.80e-14 eV; solar-split resonance energy = 380.2 MeV
  predicted survival low energy (vacuum-like): 0.55;  high energy (matter-dominated): 0.29
  Borexino measured: pp (low) 0.57 +/- 0.09; 8B (high) about 0.37 +/- 0.08 -> the environment effect is seen.
  Super-K day/night (Earth matter at night): -3.3% +/- 1.1% (2016).
  Hot plasma (early universe, supernova): neutrinos gain a thermal effective mass ~ G_F T^4 terms (Notzold & Raffelt 1988).
