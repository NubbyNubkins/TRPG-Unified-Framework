IDEA 3. The pop is the settling itself: the settling piece drives the start and hands its energy to the hidden partners
========================================================================================================
  The settling piece (mass m_s ~ settling value) decays to hidden partners with the coupling y = M_N/f that gives them their mass.
  For no hot majorons the space it heats must stay well below the partners' mass: T_reheat < M_N/10.
  Decay rate >= y^3 f / (4 pi) (it must be heavy enough to make the pair), so T_reheat / M_N ~ 0.4 sqrt(y M_red / (4 pi f)):
   y =   1e-01: partner mass  6.3e+09 GeV, T_reheat/M_N =  3.00e+02, largest CP asymmetry (Davidson-Ibarra)  6.2e-07
   y =   1e-02: partner mass  6.3e+08 GeV, T_reheat/M_N =  9.48e+01, largest CP asymmetry (Davidson-Ibarra)  6.2e-08
   y =   1e-04: partner mass  6.3e+06 GeV, T_reheat/M_N =  9.48e+00, largest CP asymmetry (Davidson-Ibarra)  6.2e-10
   y =   1e-06: partner mass  6.3e+04 GeV, T_reheat/M_N =  9.48e-01, largest CP asymmetry (Davidson-Ibarra)  6.2e-12
   y =   1e-08: partner mass  6.3e+02 GeV, T_reheat/M_N =  9.48e-02, largest CP asymmetry (Davidson-Ibarra)  6.2e-14
  Cool enough needs y below about 1e-8, i.e. a partner near 1e3 GeV, whose CP asymmetry (about 1e-13) is far too small: the matter excess
  needs (T/m) x asymmetry of about 1.6e-10, with T/m below 1. Either the start is too hot (hot majorons) or too weak (no excess).
