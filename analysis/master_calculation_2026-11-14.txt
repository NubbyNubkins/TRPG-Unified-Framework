========================================================================================================
G. COMBINATION 2: the fold's heavy state is the far-side coloured part
========================================================================================================
  The fold that closes the 2-3 quark gap needs one heavy down-type state: charge -1/3, three colours, no weak pair.
  The far-side coloured parts of the 27 are exactly that (in E6 language the D quark). So the fold adds NOTHING new: rule 6 kept.
  What this does NOT fix: the fold's direction among the three families and its strength (k = 0.12 set by the 2-3 tilt). Kept as a lead.

========================================================================================================
H. COMBINATION 3: dark matter's amount (majoron) with the matter-excess start
========================================================================================================
  theta ~ 1 (start colder than the hidden partners): full dark matter needs m = 1.42e+03 eV
  theta^2 = pi^2/3 (hot start: lepton number restored, averaged): full dark matter needs m = 131 eV
     but a hot start also fills space with thermal majorons: Omega h^2 = 0.087 extra at that mass,
     i.e. 73% of the dark matter as fast-moving (hot) majorons. Structure-formation data allow at most
     a few per cent of such hot matter (rough). The hot start and majoron dark matter do not fit together.
  Majoron lifetime to neutrinos at 0.5 keV: 1.0e+26 s (universe 4.4e17 s): stable enough.
  Non-thermal start (the end of inflation hands its energy straight to the hidden partners, Lazarides & Shafi 1991): the space never gets
  hot enough to make majorons thermally, and the partners' decays still make the matter excess. Its size is then set by one new number
  (the reheating ratio), so the matter excess becomes ALLOWED, not predicted.
