A.1 CANDIDATES FOR TARGET A, fixed before computing (each with its physical reason):
  A-i   'like with like': D and the weak pair are the two halves of ONE far-side set (the 10 of SO(10) inside the 27), so they share
        one own weight; the D is heavier only because it also carries the press link. Then D/pair = link/own = 34.5.
  A-ii  'gravity leak' (Idea tested: gravity is a byproduct of entanglement, the grain of space): the far side settles at the meeting
        (value F); the D takes its own weight from F directly; the weak pair cannot, and only gets F^2/M_Planck through the grain.
  A-iii 'pairs at the hidden neutrino partners' (like with like for the weak-charged far-side singles; an earlier test row, recomputed).
  A-iv  'one far-side piece does both jobs': the same F sets the D's own weight, the link (press) and the pair mass by leak.

==============================================================================================================
A-i like with like (D own weight = pair own weight; only the press link splits them)
==============================================================================================================
  best over every own weight from 160 GeV to 4e12 GeV: sin^2 = 0.21268 (-8.02%, -55 sigma) at 1.6e+12 GeV
  The press link gives 1.54 decades of pair-only stretch; 15.9 are needed. Even giving all three families the same link: 4.6 decades.
  all three families split by 34.5 (pairs at 1e5 GeV): sin^2 = 0.21647 (-6.38%, -43 sigma)
  RESULT A-i: FAIL. Exact consequence: the D/pair split cannot come from the link. It must sit in the OWN WEIGHTS themselves:
  the far-side colour half must weigh about 2e5 times more than the weak half before any press. That is the doublet-triplet
  splitting problem (Dimopoulos & Wilczek 1981; Masiero and others 1982), and it is now the whole of target A.

  RESULT A-ii: SURVIVES the data (1.6 sigma, unreduced Planck only). Attacks follow in section C.

C. ATTACKS ON THE SURVIVORS
==============================================================================================================
C.1 A-ii (gravity leak) against TRPG's own rules
  - In E6's 27 the weak pair and the D both couple to the settled singlet with the same three-way link (27 x 27 x 27). A rule must bar
    the pair's direct weight and allow the D's. TRPG has no such rule yet. Candidate wording: 'a weak pair has no weight of its own
    from a weak-blind piece; it can only borrow through the grain'. Not derived: this IS the doublet-triplet problem restated.
  - The leak's size has an unknown number in front (c x F^2/M_P). The rule works only with c = 1 AND the unreduced Planck mass.
    exact closure would need c = 1.47 (unreduced) or c = 0.14 (reduced). The grain (unreduced) was fixed as TRPG's footing in an earlier test,
    before this test, so choosing it is not after-the-fact; but 'c = 1' is an assumption, and any c from 0.7 to 3 would look 'natural'.
C.2 A-ii against data
  - Pairs at 1.5e8 GeV: far beyond any collider. Their neutral member must decay (mixing above 1e-15) or the start was cold: consistent.
  - Proton: no quark-to-lepton carriers on the main line (43.4); D's carry ordinary quark number: consistent.
  - The only sharp number is sin^2 itself: predicted 0.23177 +- 0.00034 against 0.23122 +- 0.00004 (+1.6 sigma).
    The error is dominated by the strong strength (+-0.00026) and the uncomputed three-loop terms (+-0.0002). When both shrink to
    about +-0.0001 (lattice strong strength; a three-loop calculation), the same central values would sit 4 to 5 sigma apart.
    So A-ii is falsifiable soon, by a calculation that needs no new experiment.
C.3 A-ii luck (an earlier test X.6): about 18 fixed rules were tried for the pair mass; a target placed anywhere has a hit as close 15% to 84%
    of the time depending on the family counted. NOT EVIDENCE on its own.
C.4 A-iv (one far-side piece does both jobs) with the data: D's own weight F, link y F, pairs F^2/M_P. The press fixes y = 34.5 x (the D's
    own coupling). With F = 4.56e+13: D at about 1.6e+15 GeV (above the meeting, so it does not disturb A-ii), pairs at 1.7e+08 GeV.
    A consistent far-side spectrum, but the 34.5 is a ratio of two couplings that nothing fixes: target B is NOT solved by A-iv.
C.5 Target B survivors: B-iv  self-similar press (chi2 5.16)
  B-iv  self-similar press: luck above (73%); its value moves with the footing (top 9.32e-04, far side 1.00e-03);
    and no mechanism in the matrix ties the far side's kept share to the near side's ladder span. NOT EVIDENCE.
