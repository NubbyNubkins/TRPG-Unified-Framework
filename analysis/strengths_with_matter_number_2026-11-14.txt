1. The gap as it stands (one loop; two loops give 0.2067, an earlier test)
====================================================================================================
  Predicted weak angle at the Z: sin^2 = 0.2076; measured 0.23122; off by -10.2%.  Meeting energy 6.8e+14 GeV.

====================================================================================================
2. Add the matter-number force above its settling energy
====================================================================================================
  What changes in the running. Above the settling energy the two single turns (hypercharge and matter number) both act. The forces' strengths
  run because of the particles they touch:
   - the matter particles: identical. Over a whole generation (hidden partner included) hypercharge and the matter-number direction orthogonal
     to it share no cross term, so their combined running equals hypercharge's alone. Checked over the 16 states:
       sum over states of (right-handed charge x matter number) = +0.000  (0 means no cross term)
   - the matter-number carrier itself: a single (abelian) carrier does not change any running.
   - the piece that settles it (the hidden partner's pairing, matter number 2, right-handed charge 1): adds (3/5)(1/3)(1) + (2/5)(1/3)(3/8)(4) = 2/5
     to the combined hypercharge running, and nothing to the strong or weak running.
     settling at  7.3e+05 GeV: sin^2 = 0.2047 (was 0.2076); off by -11.5%
     settling at  6.3e+10 GeV: sin^2 = 0.2063 (was 0.2076); off by -10.8%
     settling at  1.0e+12 GeV: sin^2 = 0.2067 (was 0.2076); off by -10.6%
     settling at  1.2e+15 GeV: sin^2 = 0.2076 (was 0.2076); off by -10.2%
  The gap gets WIDER, not narrower, whatever the settling energy.

====================================================================================================
3. Why no extra single force can close it (the attack, made general)
====================================================================================================
  The prediction misses because hypercharge, run up from the Z, reaches the weak force's strength too early. Closing the gap needs hypercharge
  to run MORE SLOWLY, or the weak force to run more slowly, or the strong force faster. A single (abelian) carrier adds nothing; every new
  piece of matter or settling field adds a POSITIVE amount to the running of every force it touches. A field touching only hypercharge and
  matter number can only speed up hypercharge. So no extension by single forces closes the gap. To close it, something must touch the weak
  or strong force: new weak pairs or coloured states between the Z and the meeting (the 4x4 alternative's right-handed force did this, an earlier test;
  so do heavier partners of every particle in supersymmetry). The main line has none (rule 6).

====================================================================================================
4. Summary
====================================================================================================
  FAIL: the matter-number force widens the gap (-10.2% -> about -10.8%). Shown in general: no single force can close it.
  SHARPENED: the strengths can only meet if something between the Z and the meeting energy touches the weak or strong force. In the main line
  that would be the six coloured far-side parts (44.2) taken as real particles below the meeting energy, or a break in rule 6.
