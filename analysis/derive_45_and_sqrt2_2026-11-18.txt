A. THE 45 DEGREES
  algebra: circulant amplitude matrix, eigenvalues sqrt(m_k) = a + 2|b| cos(delta + 2 pi k/3)
  sum sqrt m = 3a ; sum m = 3a^2 + 6|b|^2 ; Koide Q = sum m/(sum sqrt m)^2 = 1/3 + 2|b|^2/(3a^2)
  diagonal weight 3a^2, off-diagonal weight 6|b|^2  ->  p = 3a^2/(3a^2+6|b|^2)
  p = 1/2  <=>  |b|^2 = a^2/2  <=>  Q = 2/3  <=>  angle between (sqrt m) and (1,1,1) = 45 degrees   [exact]
  charged leptons (e, mu, tau) MeV                 self share p = 0.50000  Q = 0.66666  angle = 45.00 deg
  down quarks (d, s, b) at 2 GeV / m_b(m_b)        self share p = 0.45582  Q = 0.73129  angle = 47.53 deg
  up quarks (u, c, t) at 2 GeV / m_c(m_c) / m_t pole self share p = 0.39271  Q = 0.84879  angle = 51.20 deg
  neutrinos (TRPG rule) meV                        self share p = 0.63595  Q = 0.52415  angle = 37.11 deg
  tau from p = 1/2: 1776.969 MeV vs 1776.86 +- 0.12 -> +0.9 sigma
  robustness: Shannon -p ln p-(1-p)ln(1-p), Renyi of any order, and p(1-p) all peak at p = 1/2 (symmetric in p <-> 1-p): yes
  quarks: p != 1/2 (down 0.44, up 0.38 region) -> the rule as stated does not hold for quarks; colour is the named difference

B. THE GRAIN FACTOR
  gravity radius 2GM/c^2 = reduced wavelength hbar/Mc            grain = Planck / 1.414
  gravity radius 2GM/c^2 = Heisenberg minimum hbar/(2Mc)         grain = Planck / 2.000
  gravity radius 2GM/c^2 = full wavelength h/Mc                  grain = Planck / 0.564
  self-energy GM^2/R = quantum energy hbar c/R (energy balance)  grain = Planck / 1.000
  Einstein-equation coupling 8 pi G (reduced Planck)             grain = Planck / 5.013
  -> five equally natural readings of 'over-contained by its own gravity' give five factors. TRPG's ideas do not single one out.
  -> sqrt2 is NOT derived. It stays a chosen value with a physical reason (a particle becoming its own black hole); the 0.3% lattice
     alpha_s decides it (sqrt2 -> 0.1178; 1 -> 0.1196; 2 -> ~0.1166 by the earlier run's slope).
