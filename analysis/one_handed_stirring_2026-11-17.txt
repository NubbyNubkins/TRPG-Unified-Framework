1. Published falsifier: does a one-handed 0.05 nG field make too much matter at the 160 GeV step?
   Kamada & Long 2016: right matter excess at B0=1e-17 G; excess grows as B0^3 on the endpoint line; 0.05 nG overshoots by 1.2e+20
   Kamada & Long 2016: right matter excess at B0=1e-16 G; excess grows as B0^3 on the endpoint line; 0.05 nG overshoots by 1.2e+17
   Boyer & Neronov 2025 (redo, slower field decay): strong fields give excess = 0.6/(grain x T), independent of strength;
   observed excess needs grain = 9.8e+08/T = 1.8e-07 of the horizon at 130 GeV; relic today 0.2 to 100 pG (needed: 50 pG)
   So the two published calculations disagree; the idea is not dead in general.
