3. PERSPECTIVE #1, WEAK. The W matrix: each entry turns one particle's perspective into another's; energy = rate of the turn   [Fermi 1934]
========================================================================================================
  Rate of a turn from i to j: G_F^2 |V_ij|^2 m_i^5 /(192 pi^3) x (number of open ways). Every entry uses the same G_F (one strength).
  tau lifetime from the muon's (same strength, same shape; uses the measured share 17.82% for the electron route): 291.1 fs, measured 290.3 fs (+0.3%)
  top's width (top turns to bottom, |V_tb| = 1): 1.48 GeV, with the strong-force correction 1.35 GeV; measured 1.42 (+0.19 -0.15) GeV
  These are perspective turns in the plain sense: the particle stops being itself and becomes another. That is what the W entries do.

4. PERSPECTIVE #2, GRAVITY. Energy of each pair held by gravity, against what its charges hold   [Dirac 1937; Stoney 1881]
========================================================================================================
  Gravity's entry for a pair is m1 m2 / M_Planck^2 (no charge, only mass). Ratio to the electric entry alpha q1 q2:
    e, p    gravity / electric = 4.408e-40
    e, e    gravity / electric = 2.401e-43
    p, p    gravity / electric = 8.094e-37
    mu, mu  gravity / electric = 1.026e-38
  Mass at which gravity equals electric for a charge-1 pair: sqrt(alpha) M_Planck = 1.043e+18 GeV (Stoney's mass, 1881).

5. THE SHAPES. Test of the frame: what kind of matrix is each relationship?
========================================================================================================
  electric (q1 q2)          : rank 1 (a product: every pair = my number x your number; nobody changes identity)
  gravity (m1 m2)           : rank 1 (a product: every pair = my number x your number; nobody changes identity)
  Higgs link (m1 m2 / v^2)  : rank 1 (a product: every pair = my number x your number; nobody changes identity)
  W matrix (quarks)          : rank 3, rows each sum (squared) to [1. 1. 1.]: a rotation (it TURNS one into another)
  gluon (colour)             : rotation in colour (turns red into blue) AND, summed over colours, a product (the colour-blind total that binds)
  Higgs vs gravity, for elementary particles: same matrix up to one number,
    Higgs entry / gravity entry = M_Planck^2/(4 pi v^2) = 1.957e+32 for EVERY elementary pair
    (but the Higgs link reaches only 1.6e-03 fm, a thousandth of a proton; gravity reaches without limit)
  Results of the shape test:
   HOLDS  weak = perspective: the W matrix is a rotation, the only family-crossing one; its entries turn identity. Push/pull matrices never do.
   HOLDS  strong + electric = push/pull: product-shaped (colour-blind part for strong), they hold energy between partners who stay themselves.
   FAILS  gravity = perspective, in matrix form: gravity's matrix is a product (m1 m2), the push/pull shape, and changes no identity.
          It has the same shape as the Higgs link, 2e32 times weaker per pair at short range. Its perspective side (entanglement, geometry) is not visible
          in an energy matrix at all; it would have to show in which pairs are linked, not in how much energy they hold.
   HOLDS  the Z: its matrix is a product of weak charges, so the weak force has a push/pull half (Z) and a perspective half (W).

6. Summary
========================================================================================================
  Energy matrices reproduce known values well (hydrogen, positronium, muonium exact to known corrections; 14 baryons to 0.9% and 18 mesons to 2.5%, 5 numbers each;
  tau lifetime and top width from one strength). They predict pairs never seen (true muonium 1.41 keV, tauonium 23.7 keV). All of this is
  known physics. What it says about TRPG: the link value is exactly the binding factor, and the four forces sort into products (push/pull) and rotations
  (perspective turns), with gravity on the product side. No open item moved: the matrices take the masses and strengths as inputs.
