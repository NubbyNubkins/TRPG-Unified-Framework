SECTION 2. ROUTE 1: 2/9 PLUS A RADIATIVE (QED) CORRECTION
==============================================================================================================
2a. Footing A: running masses at ONE common energy mu. One-loop QED (Tarrach 1981): m(mu) = M [1 - (alpha/pi)(1 + (3/2) ln(mu/M))].
    mu = m_tau    : Q = 0.66782364 (2/3 0.174%), lean off sqrt2 by 0.173%, twist = 0.22113532 (2/9 -0.489%)
    mu = M_Z      : Q = 0.6678399 (2/3 0.176%), lean off sqrt2 by 0.176%, twist = 0.22112012 (2/9 -0.496%)
    mu = 1e16 GeV : Q = 0.66799288 (2/3 0.199%), lean off sqrt2 by 0.199%, twist = 0.22097715 (2/9 -0.56%)
    full Standard Model running to M_Z (Huang & Zhou 2021): Q = 0.66782399, twist = 0.22112939 (2/9 -0.492%)
    The mass-dependent part is mu-independent at this order: e/mu shifts by (3 alpha/2 pi) ln(m_mu/m_e) = 1.858% = 18576.5 ppm.
    Needed to turn exact (45, 2/9) into the data: +9.834 ppm.  The one-loop shift is 1889.1 times too big.  FAIL on any common-energy footing.
    (This is Sumino's 2009 point: the rules hold for the directly weighed masses, where QED says they should not, unless something cancels the log.)

2b. Footing B: each mass at its OWN scale, m_i(m_i), to two loops (Gray, Broadhurst, Grafe & Schilcher 1990, turned into QED: C_F = 1, C_A = 0, T = 1).
    two-loop numbers: d1 = -0.510562, per massless lighter lepton d3 = -1.56205, own loop d4 = 0.155351
    e  : alpha(M)/pi = 0.00232281946 (1/alpha = 137.036), K = -0.35521, m(M)/M = 0.997684471194
    mu : alpha(M)/pi = 0.00234215684 (1/alpha = 135.9046), K = -1.9054, m(M)/M = 0.997673719567
    tau: alpha(M)/pi = 0.00236298443 (1/alpha = 134.7067), K = -3.336, m(M)/M = 0.997661126005
    If exact (45, 2/9) held for m_i(m_i), the pole ratio e/mu would be the family ratio times R_mu/R_e = 1 -10.78 ppm.
    Needed: +9.834 ppm.  Got: -10.78 ppm.  Same size (alpha^2 x log), OPPOSITE sign. Miss = -20.61 ppm = -926.4 sigma. FAIL.
    The two pieces: alpha grows between the two scales: -19.34 ppm (certain); the electron loop inside the muon: +8.56 ppm (two-loop number).
    Robustness: even if the two-loop number were dropped or doubled the shift stays negative (-19.34 to -2.22 ppm). The sign cannot come out right.
    (own-scale masses: twist = 0.2222262469 with tau measured; and with 45 exact the e/mu ratio needs twist = 0.2222218551: further from 2/9, not closer.)
    Not included: hadron loops in alpha at the tau (they touch only the tau, which is not the sharp test), three-loop terms (below 1 ppm).

2c. Reverse question: what correction WOULD do it?  Suppose exact (45, 2/9) holds for m~_i = M_i (1 + c ln M_i).
    needed c = 1.8444e-6 per unit of log  = 0.34184 x (alpha/pi)^2  = 0.034636 x alpha^2  = 0.0005294 x the one-loop QED log.
    tau mass in that case: 1776.95825 MeV (differs from the 45-exact value by -10.8 keV).
    The size is that of a two-loop QED log, so a correction of this kind is not absurd. But c is one free number fitted to one datum:
    it predicts nothing until some rule gives 0.34 (alpha/pi)^2, and it also needs something to cancel the 1900-times-larger one-loop log.
    Sumino's 2009 cancellation uses a family gauge force with strength alpha/4. TRPG has no family force: the three generations are placements,
    the carriers are exactly 8|3|1, and rule 6 is 'nothing extra'. So that mechanism is not available on the main line.

3a. A theorem first (Lindemann 1882; Weierstrass 1885). If an angle in radians is a non-zero ALGEBRAIC number (any fraction is one),
    its cosine, sine and e^(i angle) are TRANSCENDENTAL. And any structure built from finite algebra (octonion structure constants,
    group characters, Clebsch-Gordan numbers, a circulant with algebraic entries) has algebraic entries and algebraic phases e^(i delta).
    So: NO finite algebraic structure can have a phase of exactly 2/9 radian. Group theory hands out rational fractions of a TURN, not of a radian.
    A rational radian can only come from a length-over-length or an integral (an area, a Berry-type phase, a loop correction).
    Check by computer: is cos(2/3) (the quantity the masses use, cos 3 delta) a root of a small polynomial?

6b. The best any gravity formula could ever do.  The Planck mass is known to 11 ppm (G to 22 ppm, CODATA 2022).

    gives M0 (equivalently m_tau). So the gravity route needs TWO rules, not one.

    In twist language: a factor 7.3 in m_e is a twist of 0.17024 instead of 0.22222.
