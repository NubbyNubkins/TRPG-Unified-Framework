6. The twist 2/9 as a ring number (three placements a third of a turn apart)
======================================================================================
  response of a ring between two seats a third of a turn apart: (1/pi^2) sum (1 - cos(2 pi n/3))/n^2 = 0.2222222  (2/9 = 0.2222222)
  same thing in plain terms: two seats split the ring into arcs of 1/3 and 2/3; the two paths in parallel give
  (1/3 x 2/3)/(1/3 + 2/3) = 0.2222222 = 2/9.  For N seats: (1/N)(1 - 1/N).
  measured lepton twist / (2/9) = 1.0000114 +- 0.0000282; so the unit ('one full ring = one radian') holds to about 1 part in 10^5,
  but that unit is NOT derived (Shulga 2026 calls the same normalization an undetermined matching coefficient).
  quark triple: three colour links in a row add: 3 x 2/9 = 0.6667; measured 0.6499 +- 0.0011 (the charm offset again).

7. Why 3 generations makes the two lepton rules agree
======================================================================================
  N = 2: 'self = cross' gives Q = 2/N = 1.0000;  ring fraction left over 1 - 1/N = 0.5000  
  N = 3: 'self = cross' gives Q = 2/N = 0.6667;  ring fraction left over 1 - 1/N = 0.6667  <- equal only here
  N = 4: 'self = cross' gives Q = 2/N = 0.5000;  ring fraction left over 1 - 1/N = 0.7500  
  N = 5: 'self = cross' gives Q = 2/N = 0.4000;  ring fraction left over 1 - 1/N = 0.8000  
  With 3 generations the balance number (2/3) and the ring's remaining fraction (2/3) coincide, so twist = Q/3 = 2/9 is one statement, not two.
