B. Same wave in three placements = circulant (Z3) relation matrix
==============================================================================
trial 0: eigenvalues [0.305933 0.529776 0.738428]; Brannen form [0.305933 0.529776 0.738428]; max match error 2.2e-16; Fourier diagonalises (off-diag 2.1e-16)

So: any 3x3 relation matrix that is unchanged by moving every member one place round (Z3)
has eigenvalues a + 2|b| cos(arg b + 2 pi k/3): Brannen's formula, with twist = arg b, lean set by |b|/a.
Lean 45 deg <=> 3a^2 (self weight) = 6|b|^2 (cross weight): tan^2(lean) = cross/self.
  leptons                lean 45.000 deg  cross/self weight 1.000  twist 0.2222
  down quarks (d,s,b)    lean 47.535 deg  cross/self weight 1.194  twist 0.1101
  up quarks (u,c,t)      lean 51.195 deg  cross/self weight 1.546  twist 0.0745
Content check: 3 placements alone fit ANY three masses (3 numbers: scale, lean, twist).
The physics is in the values: leptons have cross = self exactly (to 1e-5) and twist 2/9.

==============================================================================
D. 'Three different shapes' (point, line, sheet) reading
==============================================================================
In 3D, a localised disturbance can be a point (codim 3), a line (codim 2) or a sheet (codim 1): exactly 3 kinds.
But their energies scale as size^0, size^1, size^2 and they carry DIFFERENT conserved charges and spins
(Kibble 1976; Vilenkin & Shellard 1994). Generations carry IDENTICAL charges and spin 1/2 in every family:
  (Q, T3, colour, spin) for e, mu, tau: {'e': (-1, -0.5, 0, 0.5), 'mu': (-1, -0.5, 0, 0.5), 'tau': (-1, -0.5, 0, 0.5)} -> identical.
Verdict: 'three different shapes' FAILS. Whatever the three ways are, they must be the same shape.

E. Excited-states reading: one level law?
==============================================================================
  leptons  power law n^k: k(1->2)= 7.69, k(2->3)= 6.96;  ratios    206.8,     16.8
  down     power law n^k: k(1->2)= 4.31, k(2->3)= 9.37;  ratios     19.9,     44.7
  up       power law n^k: k(1->2)= 9.20, k(2->3)=12.11;  ratios    589.4,    135.6
No single law within a family, and different between families. A tower also implies a 4th level:
  Z width N_nu = 2.9963 +/- 0.0074 (LEP), and a 4th heavy chiral family is excluded by Higgs rates (LHC).
  An excited muon would decay mu -> e gamma: MEG II (2025) BR < 1.5e-13. Composite leptons: scale > ~10-20 TeV (LEP).
Verdict: a simple excitation tower is disfavoured (not strictly excluded: models can hide it).

F. Octonion-valued relation matrices: only up to 3x3 are consistent
==============================================================================
  quaternion 2x2: Jordan identity error = 3.6e-15  -> consistent
  quaternion 3x3: Jordan identity error = 2.8e-14  -> consistent
  quaternion 4x4: Jordan identity error = 2.8e-14  -> consistent
  octonion   2x2: Jordan identity error = 1.4e-14  -> consistent
  octonion   3x3: Jordan identity error = 2.8e-14  -> consistent
  octonion   4x4: Jordan identity error = 2.3e+02  -> FAILS
Theorem (Jordan, von Neumann & Wigner 1934): octonionic Hermitian matrices form a consistent algebra only up to 3x3
(the 27-dimensional Albert algebra). Proposed link to 3 generations: Dubois-Violette 2016; Todorov & Dubois-Violette 2018;
Boyle 2020. Link to generations is a published proposal, not proven.
