5. The Higgs link as the third force: its charge is mass
====================================================================================================
  Reach = 1.58e-03 fm = 1/534 of the proton's radius. At the size of an atom it is cut by a factor e^-3.4e+07. No leftover outside.
     pull between two of the electron: strength 3.4e-13 (electric: 7.3e-03)
     pull between two of the top quark: strength 3.9e-02 (electric: 7.3e-03)
  Can it bind? A short-reach pull binds two equal particles only if strength x (half their mass) / carrier mass > 0.8399 (Yukawa 1935).
     The Higgs link would bind particles heavier than about 543 GeV; the heaviest, the top, has 32% of that mass.
     The weak force would need about 3994 GeV. Neither binds anything that exists.
     So the pattern holds: the two facing-blind forces bind; the weak force and the Higgs link only change things (identity; mass).
     ATTACK: the reason they do not bind is their short reach, and the short reach is the Higgs side settling. So 'sees the facing' and
     'cannot bind' are tied through test 2, not independently.
  Is mass-as-charge the same as gravity's charge? Gravity: every kind of matter falls alike to 1.5 parts in 10^15 (MICROSCOPE 2022).
     Higgs charge per unit of mass: electron 1.00; proton about 0.29 (Shifman, Vainshtein & Zakharov 1978), because 99% of the proton is balance
     energy that the Higgs link does not see. FAIL: the Higgs link is not gravity. They share one thing only: a single sign, so nothing cancels.
     Coincidence, NOT a result: the heavy quarks' share of the proton's Higgs charge is exactly 2/9 at first order; TRPG's twist is 2/9.
     One comes from counting colours and light quarks (2 x 3 / 27), the other is an angle. No link is known. Recorded so it is not rediscovered.

6. The third facing and the three families
====================================================================================================
  The three-way product on the WHOLE matrix (27 parts). For a random element x, the operation 'x, x, .' has values with multiplicities [1, 8, 1, 8, 8, 1].
     Three single values [0.41, 17.6227, 66.8139] and three eightfold ones [9.0164, 33.612, 42.2183];
     each eightfold value is the average of two single ones (error 3e-14).
     The same on the 16 link states alone: multiplicities [4, 1, 4, 6, 1] (two sizes).
  -> A relationship matrix with octonion entries, read three-way, has exactly THREE independent sizes: never four, because no larger such matrix
     exists (Jordan, von Neumann & Wigner 1934; Albert 1934; Cartan 1935; Loos 1977). From one spot the observer sees two of them.
  TRPG's own rule 5 already has this shape: the three lepton masses are the three sizes of ONE 3x3 matrix, at three angles 120 degrees apart
     (the 'third facing' as an angle): sizes [0.0403, 0.5802, 2.3794]; squared ratios 0.004836 and 0.059460;
     measured electron/muon 0.004836, muon/tau 0.059461. (Koide 1982; Brannen 2006; an earlier test. The twist 2/9 is measured, an earlier test.)
  TEST: 'the three families are the three spots of the one matrix' (the observer's third choice). Each spot sees 16 link states, so three
     families need 48. The matrix has 3 links of 8, each written with an i: 24. Each link would be shared by two families. FAIL by a factor 2.
     (From any one spot the third link is not matter at all: it is on the far side, with the Higgs numbers.)
  TEST: 'the Higgs link tells the families apart' in the main line as it stands (three placements, an earlier test). The 12 turns and the three-way
     product act the same on every placement and put no condition on how the link differs between them. It is free: that is where the
     measured rules 5 and 7, the heavy quark masses, the mixing tilt and the twist all sit. NOT DERIVED.
  -> 'No more than three': the matrix gives 'at most three sizes' as a theorem. It becomes 'at most three families' only if a family IS a size
     of one matrix, and one matrix cannot hold both the forces and the families (24 against 48). So it is a strong hint that the 3 of the
     families and the 3 of the matrix are the same 3, and it is NOT a derivation. The item stays open, resting on rule 6 and data.

7. The switch tested: 'the Higgs counts all energy because it is a constant; gravity supplies much more of the mass'
====================================================================================================
  The idea behind it, taken in two halves and tested against measurement.
  HALF 1: the Higgs field is a constant filling space (true: 246 GeV everywhere), so it should count ALL energy.
     Test: most of a proton's energy is gluon energy. A field that counted all of it would link to gluon energy 11.5 times more strongly
     than the Higgs is predicted to (which sees gluons only through the top quark). The LHC makes Higgs particles mostly from gluons, so
     the rate would be 132 times higher. Measured rate / standard prediction: 1.0 +/- 0.1 (ATLAS and CMS 2022). FAIL by a factor of about 132.
     (Georgi, Glashow, Machacek & Nanopoulos 1978; Shifman, Vainshtein & Zakharov 1978; Goldberger, Grinstein & Skiba 2008.)
     Second test: switch off the Higgs link to the up and down quarks, and the proton still weighs about 880 of its 938 MeV (94%).
     The proton's mass computed from the strong force alone agrees with measurement to about 2% (Duerr et al. 2008).
     Correction to my own '1%': the Higgs's share of the proton is 1% (up and down masses), about 9% (with the strange quark), or 29%
     if the heavy quarks' indirect effect is counted. So 1% undersold it. It is still far from all.
  HALF 2: gravity supplies much more of the mass.
     As a force, no: the proton's own gravity holds 1.4e-39 GeV, which is 1e-39 of its mass.
     As a statement about where the mass comes from, it holds as a READING in TRPG's own terms: 99% of the proton is balance energy, which
     is relationship energy (an earlier test; an earlier test), and gravity is the byproduct of relationship (entanglement). The proton's mass is
     defined in physics as exactly what gravity counts (the trace of its energy and stress). So most of the mass is relationship energy and
     only a small part comes from the Higgs link. NOT SHOWN: that the entanglement which gives gravity and the colour balance which gives
     the proton's mass are the same relationship. That would be a real result if it could be shown, and it is not shown here.
  WHAT IS CONSTANT AND COUNTS EVERYTHING: not the Higgs field but the kept amount of the bag (dark energy, 44.5), and gravity itself.
  -> The switch as stated FAILS on its first half (measured, a factor of about 130). Its second half holds as a reading, not as a calculation.
     Restated: gravity counts all the energy; most of that energy is relationship energy; the Higgs link adds between 1% and 29% of a proton.
