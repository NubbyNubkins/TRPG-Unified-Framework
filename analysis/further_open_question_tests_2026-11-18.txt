2. WHAT PUSHES QUARKS OFF THE 45 DEG LEPTON CONE?
   shift down 3.150 deg, up 7.147 deg, ratio 2.269 +- 0.012
   shift proportional to |charge| (1/3 : 2/3): needs 2.0, measured 2.269 -> +22 sigma -> FAIL
   shift proportional to charge^2: needs 4.0, measured 2.269 -> -142 sigma -> FAIL
   in Koide-Q terms: excess down 0.0822, up 0.2186, ratio 2.66 (neither 2 nor 4)
   The D fold presses DOWN quarks only, yet UP quarks sit farther from 45 deg:
   so the fold is not what moves quarks off the lepton cone. FAIL for that reading.

4. MATTER PARITY (-1)^{3(B-L)} vs MMG DECAY
   16 quarks (B-L=1/3)            parity odd
   16 leptons (B-L=-1)            parity odd
   10 weak pairs, MMG (B-L=0)     parity even
   10 coloured D (B-L=+-2/3)      parity even
   1 singlet (B-L=0)              parity even
   A fermion that is EVEN cannot decay to an odd number of SM fermions (all odd) plus bosons:
   with the parity exact, the lightest even fermion (neutral MMG) is STABLE, and MMG-lepton or D-down
   mixing (needed for MMG decay and for the D fold) is FORBIDDEN. The exact parity and those two pieces contradict.
   stable neutral MMG as dark matter: sigma_n ~ 2.3e-39 cm^2 vs direct-detection limit ~ 2e-41 at 2.5e+08 GeV
   -> excluded by ~93x (order of magnitude). Mass splitting from v^2/F = 1.3 eV, far below ~100 keV: stays Dirac.
