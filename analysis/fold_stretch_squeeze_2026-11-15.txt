
========================================================================================================
1. What the fold is, geometrically
========================================================================================================
  M -> M - k M0 u u^T shrinks the shape along one direction u by the fraction k (k < 0 stretches it), leaving the two directions
  across u untouched. So the 'expansion and shrinkage' and the fold are one operation: a squeeze or stretch of the hidden matrix
  along one direction. A turn cannot change the mass ratios; a squeeze can, and it changes the turn between the ladders too.

========================================================================================================
2. Squeeze and stretch along u = (-1,-1,2), one number
========================================================================================================
  V1 down squeezed only                   : amount -0.1162  1-2 tilt 0.2664 (1.18x)  1-3 tilt 0.00445 (1.19x)  worst 1.19x
  V2 both squeezed equally                : no amount gives the measured 2-3 tilt
  V3 down squeezed, up stretched equally  : amount +0.0660  1-2 tilt 0.2400 (1.07x)  1-3 tilt 0.00455 (1.22x)  worst 1.22x
