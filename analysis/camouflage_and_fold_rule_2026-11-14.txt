1. When do identical particles show as ONE wave? (the picture, made exact)
========================================================================================================
  Identical particles that can share a state (bosons: Bose 1924, Einstein 1925) pile into one wave; identical particles that cannot (fermions,
  Pauli 1925) always stay separate. So 'two identical waves look like one wave' is exact for bosons. They merge when more than one particle
  sits inside one wavelength-sized box: occupation N = (density / mass) x (wavelength)^3 > 1.
   mass    1e-22 eV: wavelength  1.69e+21 cm, particles per wavelength-box  1.93e+94  -> ONE WAVE
   mass    1e-20 eV: wavelength  1.69e+19 cm, particles per wavelength-box  1.93e+86  -> ONE WAVE
   mass    1e-06 eV: wavelength  1.69e+05 cm, particles per wavelength-box  1.93e+30  -> ONE WAVE
   mass    1e+00 eV: wavelength  1.69e-01 cm, particles per wavelength-box  1.93e+06  -> ONE WAVE
   mass    1e+01 eV: wavelength  1.69e-02 cm, particles per wavelength-box  1.93e+02  -> ONE WAVE
   mass    3e+01 eV: wavelength  5.63e-03 cm, particles per wavelength-box  2.38e+00  -> ONE WAVE
   mass    1e+03 eV: wavelength  1.69e-04 cm, particles per wavelength-box  1.93e-06  -> separate particles
   mass    1e+09 eV: wavelength  1.69e-10 cm, particles per wavelength-box  1.93e-30  -> separate particles
  Any dark matter BOSON lighter than about 37 eV is camouflaged in this sense: its particles overlap into one wave everywhere in a galaxy.
  This is a real, studied class ('wave dark matter': Hu, Barkana & Gruzinov 2000; Hui et al. 2017). The famous axion is one (1e-6 eV).
  Checks: (a) it is still dark: overlap hides the particles' individuality, not their mass, so the pull is unchanged; it must also carry no charge,
  or it would shine. (b) Fermions cannot do this at all, and fermion dark matter must be heavier than ~100 eV to fit into small galaxies
  (Tremaine & Gunn 1979). (c) The lightest end (1e-22 eV, waves the size of a galaxy's core) is now disfavoured by the Lyman-alpha forest:
  mass must exceed ~2e-20 eV (Rogers & Peiris 2021). Between ~1e-20 eV and ~30 eV the idea is fully open. HOLDS as a physical possibility.

2. Is there a wave-type (spin 0, chargeless, long-lived) piece already in the main line?
========================================================================================================
  an earlier test: the hidden neutrino partner's own mass is the one mass that needs no Higgs; the piece that settles it carries matter number 2.
  Reading (ii) of an earlier test (matter number a real force above ~1e11 GeV): that piece's phase is eaten by the force's carrier. Nothing left.
  Reading (i) (the spot is kept exactly, matter number never a force): the same piece's phase is LEFT OVER as a spin-0, chargeless, near-massless
  wave: the majoron (Chikashige, Mohapatra & Peccei 1981). It is not an extra state; it is part of a piece the main line already has.
   majoron mass   1e-20 eV: lifetime ~  5.1e+48 s (universe 4.4e17 s) -> stable enough; one wave
   majoron mass   1e-06 eV: lifetime ~  5.1e+34 s (universe 4.4e17 s) -> stable enough; one wave
   majoron mass   1e+00 eV: lifetime ~  5.1e+28 s (universe 4.4e17 s) -> stable enough; one wave
   majoron mass   1e+01 eV: lifetime ~  5.1e+27 s (universe 4.4e17 s) -> stable enough; one wave
   majoron mass   1e+03 eV: lifetime ~  5.1e+25 s (universe 4.4e17 s) -> stable enough; separate particles
  So reading (i) gives a camouflaged dark matter candidate that rule 6 allows (an earlier test said rule 6 forbade an extra state; this is not extra).
  NOT derived: its mass (set by tiny symmetry-breaking effects, e.g. gravity) and how much of it the early universe made. Those decide whether it
  is ALL the dark matter. Studied in the literature (Berezinsky & Valle 1993; Garcia-Cely & Heeck 2017). Label: CANDIDATE, open.
  Price: choosing reading (i) gives up the matter-number force (reading ii), which nothing measured needs. That is a choice.
