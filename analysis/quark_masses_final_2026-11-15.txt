2. (a, c) WHAT E6 ITSELF SAYS ABOUT PAIRS AND SINGLES: the singles have a far-side twin, the pairs do not
====================================================================================================
In the 27 the right-side down partner (a single) has a twin of the same charges in the 10 (D's partner); the right-side up partner
(a pair) has none. With the reverse block empty (Nelson 1984; Barr 1984; Bento, Branco & Parada 1991) the heavy D mixes only the
right-side singles, and the light down matrix becomes   H_d = m (1 - kappa u u^dagger) m^T,   kappa = |F|^2/(|F|^2 + M_D^2),
with m the real base, F the far-side link (direction u) and M_D the D's own weight. This is a press from the RIGHT side.

2.1 One 27x27x27 link matrix for the base, the far-side link and D's weight (three D's, one per family): the press is 0.7569 x the unit matrix,
    largest off-diagonal tilt 4.2e-12. Exactly no tilt and identical ladder shapes: FAIL. A second, independent link matrix is needed,
    and its entries are new free numbers (this is Bento, Branco & Parada's minimal model). E6 does not supply them.

2.2 Same bubble for both ladders (down base = c x up matrix), one D pressing the singles. In the up ladder's own basis
    H_d = c^2 D_u (1 - kappa w w^dagger) D_u = c^2 D_u^2 - v v^dagger: a rank-one change. Its eigenvectors are v_i/(lam_i - lam'_j)
    (Loewner 1934; Golub 1973), so every phase of w drops out: J = 0 EXACTLY, whatever the timing. The masses fix |w| for each c,
    leaving ONE free number (c) for the four tilts.
    interlacing (Cauchy) allows c only between m_d/m_u = 2.156 and m_b/m_c = 4.576.
    best c = 3.139: tilts [0.0532 0.6858 0.     0.0365] (measured [0.225  0.0418 0.0037 0.0086]), worst 192.5x, 1 - kappa = 1.1e-08, J = 0.
    FAIL: the two bubbles are different shapes before the press (as 81.5 found for the other form of the press).
    With all three D's pressing, 1 - K is any 3x3 matrix (9 numbers for 7 measured): it fits everything and predicts nothing.
