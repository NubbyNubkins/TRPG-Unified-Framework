3. Checks at the meeting point and on the pairs themselves
==============================================================================================================
Meeting: 4.56e+13 GeV, 1/alpha = 39.0. Above it the three lines part again unless the theory changes there (no merged force in the main line).
  Carried on to the grain (unreduced Planck mass) with the whole far side counted: 1/alpha = 26.7 (hypercharge), 41.1 (weak), 48.8 (strong)
If carriers that turn quarks into leptons sat at the meeting (as in Georgi-Glashow): proton lifetime about M^4/(alpha^2 m_p^5) = 1.9e+26 years
  (rough to a factor 10). Super-Kamiokande 2020: p -> e+ pi0 longer than 2.4e34 years. FAIL by a factor 1e+08.
  A meeting with such carriers must sit above about 4.8e+15 GeV. So the main line survives ONLY because it has no such carriers (43.4).
  Gravity alone (Planck-suppressed): about 6e+44 years. No signal expected at Hyper-Kamiokande.

The pairs: a charged member and a neutral member. Loops make the charged one heavier by about 0.34 GeV (Thomas & Wells 1998;
  Cirelli, Fornengo & Strumia 2006), so it decays to the neutral one and a pion in about 1e-11 s. Does the neutral one decay?
  It has the same charges as the ordinary lepton pair, so it can mix with it, exactly as the D mixes with the down quarks (the fold).
    mixing link 1e-06: lifetime 1.5e-19 s
    mixing link 1e-10: lifetime 1.5e-11 s
    mixing link 1e-14: lifetime 1.5e-03 s
  To be gone before the light elements form (0.1 s) the link need only exceed 1e-15. Any fold-like mixing does it.
  If the neutral one were exactly stable and the early universe was ever hotter than M_L: leftover = 0.12 x (M_L / 1.1 TeV)^2 = 5e+09
    against the measured dark matter 0.120: too much by 4e+10. (Any stable thermal relic above about 1e5 GeV overcloses: Griest & Kamionkowski 1990.)
    And a Z-coupled relic scatters on nuclei at about 1e-39 cm^2; LZ 2024 allows about 2e-41 cm^2 at this mass (scaled linearly). Excluded as the dark matter.
  So: either the neutral member decays (fold-like mixing), or the start was colder than about M_L/20 = 1e+07 GeV.
  The cold, non-thermal start of an earlier test (combination 3) already satisfies the second; the fold-like mixing satisfies the first. No conflict.
  Light-element formation (BBN) and the microwave background: no effect; the pairs are gone or were never made. Colliders: out of reach by 4 decades.
