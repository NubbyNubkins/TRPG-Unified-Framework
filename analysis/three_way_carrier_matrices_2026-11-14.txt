A. What the three-way relations already give
====================================================================================================
  A1. The Higgs-W-W and Higgs-Z-Z meetings tie the W and Z masses to one Higgs value: W/Z = cos(weak angle) at first look.
      From the Z mass and the weak angle: W = 79.95 GeV; measured 80.37 (-0.52%). Adding the known loop corrections gives 80.357 (PDG 2024).
  A2. That tie (rho = W^2 / (Z^2 cos^2) = 1) holds only if the Higgs piece is a weak PAIR of charge 1/2. A weak triple would give rho = 1/2, or no
      Z mass at all (Ross & Veltman 1975). The main line's far side holds exactly such a pair and no other colourless piece.
      So the main line PREDICTS rho = 1 at first look. Measured, with known loops removed: 1.00031 +/- 0.00019 (PDG 2024). HOLDS
      (known physics, now coming from the matrix's own far side; the 0.0003 is allowed for any tiny extra piece, not required).
  A3. Every carrier meeting cancels its own 'leak' only if each family's charges sum correctly (Adler 1969; Bell & Jackiw 1969; Georgi & Glashow 1972).
      The main line's 16 states pass every such sum. HOLDS. This is what fixes the up partner at -2/3 and the electron partner at +1.
  A4. The W's matrix is the only one that crosses families; photon, Z, gluon and Higgs never do (at first look). That is why mixing shows only in
      W meetings and why no Z ever turns a strange quark into a down quark directly (measured: K-long to two muons is 7 in a billion of its decays, the size loops alone give). HOLDS (known).

C. Does any three-way relation fix the open numbers?
====================================================================================================
  The open numbers are entries of two of these matrices: the Higgs matrix (the masses: bottom, charm, strange, top) and the W matrix (the tilt).
  Three kinds of three-way relation are known that could fix them. Each tested:
  C1. BALANCE. The Higgs is pushed by every meeting it is part of: by the W, Z and itself one way, by the top (and the other matter) the other way.
      If the pushes balance exactly, the Higgs's own mass is protected (Veltman 1981). That is a push/pull condition across ALL the Higgs triples:
         Higgs^2 + 2 W^2 + Z^2 = 36,909 GeV^2      4 x (sum of matter masses^2, colours counted) = 119,202 GeV^2      ratio 0.310
      Read as a prediction: Higgs = 313 GeV (measured 125.2), or top = 96.0 GeV (measured 172.6).
      FAIL at our energy. Does it balance at some higher energy? Follow every strength up (one loop, Standard Model):
         at    1e+03 GeV: balance = -0.962   (0 = balanced; at the top's own energy -1.157)
         at    1e+08 GeV: balance = -0.459   (0 = balanced; at the top's own energy -1.157)
         at    1e+12 GeV: balance = -0.309   (0 = balanced; at the top's own energy -1.157)
         at    1e+16 GeV: balance = -0.210   (0 = balanced; at the top's own energy -1.157)
         at    1e+19 GeV: balance = -0.148   (0 = balanced; at the top's own energy -1.157)
      Never balances below gravity's grain at one loop; it is closest at the grain (-0.148). With two loops published work puts the balance
      point at or somewhat above the grain (reported by Hamada, Kawai & Oda 2013; Masina & Quiros 2013). So 'the Higgs is balanced where gravity takes over' is
      a live reading. ATTACK: it is ONE condition, so at most it fixes ONE number (it ties the Higgs mass to the top's, the same tie as 44.4's
      'gravity's grain sets the Higgs', which missed at 2.6 sigma). It says nothing about bottom, charm, strange or the tilt. NOT a fix for our items.
  C2. ZEROS. Suppose some entries of the Higgs matrix are exactly zero before the W picks its directions (Fritzsch 1977, 1978). Then the W's mixing
      follows from the masses. The simplest pattern ('six zeros') predicts, at one energy (the top's):
         |V_us| between 0.1795 and 0.2685      measured 0.2250   -> inside: HOLDS (Gatto, Sartori & Tonin 1968; already in an earlier test)
         |V_cb| between 0.0741 and 0.1984      measured 0.0418   -> below the lowest value by a factor 1.8: FAIL (known since the top was found)
         |V_ub/V_cb| = sqrt(up/charm) = 0.0445         measured 0.0892  -> 0.50 x: FAIL
      Richer patterns ('four zeros', Fritzsch & Xing 2000) still fit, but each adds as many free numbers as it explains. They relate mixing to masses;
      they never produce a mass. FAIL as a fix.
  C3. EQUALITY. Suppose all Higgs entries are equal at one energy (the matrix's own reading, an earlier test). Follow top, bottom and tau up (one loop):
         at  1.7e+02 GeV: top 0.937   bottom 0.0158   tau 0.0101   bottom/tau 1.57   top/bottom 59
         at  1.0e+08 GeV: top 0.614   bottom 0.0091   tau 0.0103   bottom/tau 0.88   top/bottom 67
         at  2.9e+16 GeV: top 0.439   bottom 0.0060   tau 0.0097   bottom/tau 0.62   top/bottom 73
         at  1.2e+19 GeV: top 0.405   bottom 0.0055   tau 0.0093   bottom/tau 0.59   top/bottom 74
      Bottom equals tau at one energy, about 2e+06 GeV (a known near-coincidence; it does not hold at the meeting energy: 0.62).
      But the top stays 59 to 74 times the bottom at every energy. 'All Higgs entries equal' never happens. FAIL.
  C4. STRENGTHS. The carrier strengths (e, g, g', g_s) are the photon, W, Z and gluon matrices' overall sizes. Only the meeting-point relation ties
      them: 10.6% off. The three-way matrices add no new relation among them. FAIL as a fix.

D. Summary
====================================================================================================
  BUILT  : the five carrier matrices, carrier-carrier triples and four-way meetings (TRPG_Three_Way_Carrier_Matrices.xlsx).
  HOLDS  : three-way relations give real values: the W/Z mass tie, rho = 1 from the main line's own Higgs pair, and every charge (cancellation sums).
  HOLDS  : the matrices hold values never measured (8 entries, plus 4 with limits only). All are fixed by measured numbers: predictions, not answers.
  FAILS  : as a fix for the open numbers. The open masses ARE the Higgs matrix's entries and the tilt IS the W matrix; none of the three known kinds of
           three-way relation (balance, zeros, equality) fixes them. Balance can fix at most one number and points to gravity's grain.
  So the three-way matrices are the right PLACE (they say exactly where every open number sits) but they do not yet give the RULE.
